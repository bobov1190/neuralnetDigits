Each map shows which of your pixels pushed the model toward its answer (red) and which pushed toward the runner-up (blue). White means the pixel did not matter. For softmax this is exact: the numbers add up to the gap between the two scores. For the neural network it is an approximation (gradient × input).

What softmax learned: 10 templates

Softmax keeps 64 weights per digit. Drawn as a picture, they are the digit's template: ink on a red pixel votes FOR this digit, ink on a blue pixel votes AGAINST it. The scores are just these templates laid over your drawing.
